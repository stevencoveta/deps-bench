Both 5/5, no gate violations in either arm. Notes:

- The naive arm was verified by this bench's external verifier. A real naive cron has
  no verifier: its output is an unreviewed diff and a claim. Instance 4's naive run
  reported success it could only know from a background-session read; without the
  harness's process sessions a plain cron loses exactly that run.
- Single trial per instance. Reliability (pass@k over 10 trials) is the next
  measurement; a single pass proves feasibility, not dependability.
- kimi-k3 did not attempt test-tampering in either arm on any instance. The trap
  needs adversarial pressure (weaker models, more broken instances, time pressure)
  to measure the false-green rate meaningfully.
